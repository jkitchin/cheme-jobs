## Federal labs and fellowships

The only fixed deadlines in the current set, and the only category where
citizenship is a categorical bar rather than a sponsorship preference. SULI and
NSF REU require US citizenship or permanent residency with no exceptions, and
the eligibility rules are checked mechanically — GPA, credit hours on the
transcript *as of the deadline*, lifetime application caps. If you are eligible
and you do one thing this month, it is SULI.

### US Dept. of Energy, Office of Science — Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI), Spring 2027
`One application · 17 participating labs incl. ORNL, Argonne, PNNL, NREL, LBNL, Brookhaven, Ames, Fermilab, SLAC, Sandia`

A paid research appointment placing undergraduates with a staff mentor at a
national laboratory. ChemE-relevant tracks run through energy science, physical
sciences, isotopes, separations and catalysis. One application covers every
participating lab, which makes this the highest-leverage form you will fill out
this fall. The eligibility gate is unusually literal: your credit hours and GPA
must appear on a transcript **by the deadline**, not by the start of the term,
and there is a lifetime cap of two SULI internships and four applications ever.
DOE is running applicant office hours on 9, 16 and 23 September, and Argonne
runs its own application-assistance workshop.

[science.osti.gov/wdts/suli](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli) · [How to Apply](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli/How-to-Apply) · [Eligibility](https://science.osti.gov/wdts/suli/Eligibility) · [Argonne's page](https://www.anl.gov/education/science-undergraduate-laboratory-internship)

### Oak Ridge National Laboratory — Research Student Internships (RSI), Spring 2027
`Oak Ridge, TN (on-site or virtual) · via ORISE/Zintellect`

The main non-SULI research route into ORNL, matching students to mentors across
the lab's science areas. This is a separate application from SULI with a later
deadline, so it is a genuine second shot rather than a duplicate. The stipend
package is the best on this board: an explicit weekly rate that scales with
degree level, plus a housing allowance on top and travel reimbursement if you
are relocating. Recent graduates and veterans are eligible on defined windows,
which is unusual.

The furthest along of any sector — Phillips 66, Marathon and ConocoPhillips all

## Semiconductors and electronic materials

The fastest-growing employer of chemical engineers, and the sector where ChemEs
are hardest to find by job title — fabs hire you as a **process engineer,
process integration (PID), yield enhancement (YED), thin films, etch, CMP or
diffusion** engineer, never as a "chemical engineer". TSMC Arizona has Summer
2027 requisitions live and lists ChemE explicitly. Watch for export-control
language on anything defence-adjacent.

An umbrella requisition covering process engineering, process integration and

## How to use this board

- **The federal deadlines are the only real ones.** NASA Spring 2027 on 14
  September, SULI on 30 September, ORNL RSI on 28 October. Everything else is
  rolling or unposted. If you are a US citizen or LPR with a 3.0, these three
  applications are the highest expected value on this page.
- **For industry, "apply in the first two weeks of the posting" beats any
  deadline.** Refining, chemicals and consumer-products programmes review
  rolling and fill in the fall; a posted closing date is the last possible day,
  not the day decisions get made.
- **Career fairs in September and October are the actual funnel** for oil and
  gas, refining, chemicals and consumer products. Apply on the company site *and*
  through the campus portal — recruiters cross-check both.
- **Sort by the citizenship filter first.** It is the most common hard
  disqualifier in this field. Every DOE and NASA programme, and nearly every
  refining employer, requires citizenship or permanent residency and will not
  sponsor. If you need sponsorship, the productive targets are the semiconductor
  and pharma sectors, BASF's international programme, and university research.
- **Search job titles that are not "chemical engineer".** Process engineer,
  process integration, yield, thin films, plant technical, technical services,
  process development, drug substance, MSAT, technical operations. The sectors
  with the most ChemE headcount are the ones that never use the phrase.
- **Watch out for the four traps on this board:** requisitions that require a
  prior internship at the same company (Lilly's Intern-to-Intern, Chevron
  returner reqs), undated requisitions where the year is ambiguous
  (Eastman, LyondellBasell), 2026-labelled postings that read as current, and
  evergreen reqs that have quietly stopped accepting applicants (ExxonMobil's
  PhD req).
- **Set a February reminder now** for REUs, NASA Summer 2027 and NIST SURF. The
  spring cycle is easy to miss entirely after a fall spent on industry
  applications.
