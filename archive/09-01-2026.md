## Federal labs, fellowships and pipeline programs

This is the only section with dates that will not move, and the only one where
citizenship is a categorical bar rather than a sponsorship preference. SULI,
NASA OSTEM, ORNL and NIST SURF all require US citizenship or lawful permanent
residency and check it before anything else — no waivers, no export-control
workaround. The one genuine exception is NETL's Professional Internship Program,
which has no blanket citizenship restriction and a 2.5 GPA floor, making it the
most accessible federal research route on this board for an international
student or a student with a middling transcript.

### NASA — Office of STEM Engagement (OSTEM) Internships, Spring 2027 and Summer 2027
`All NASA centers · apply via NASA STEM Gateway`

A single application feeds every NASA center and is matched to individual
mentor-led projects; ChemE-relevant work runs through propulsion, life support
and environmental control, in-situ resource utilization, materials and thermal
systems. Spring and fall sessions are 15 weeks, summer is 10 — the spring
session is a full academic term, so treat it as a co-op you take instead of a
semester, not something you bolt on. The trap is timing rather than eligibility:
the Spring 2027 deadline is inside two weeks, and if you miss it the next entry
point is not until the Summer 2027 deadline of 26 February 2027. Recent
graduates stay eligible for three sessions after graduating, which is unusually
generous.

### US Dept. of Energy, Office of Science — Science Undergraduate Laboratory Internships (SULI), Spring 2027
`One application · participating DOE national laboratories`

A paid research appointment placing an undergraduate with a staff mentor at a
national laboratory; the ChemE-dense tracks are separations, catalysis, isotope
production, energy storage and materials. One application covers every
participating lab, which makes this the single highest-leverage form a US-citizen
undergraduate will fill out this fall. Two traps, both mechanical. First, the
credit hours and GPA must appear **on a transcript submitted by the deadline** —
AP credit earned in high school does not count toward the 6 STEM / 12 total
credit-hour minimums. Second, there is a lifetime cap: two completed SULI
internships and four applications ever, so a scattershot application is a wasted
one. DOE is running applicant office hours on 9, 16 and 23 September at 2:00 p.m.
ET, which is the cheapest hour you can spend on this.

### National Energy Technology Laboratory — Professional Internship Program (PIP)
`Pittsburgh, PA · Morgantown, WV · Albany, OR · NETL-PIP via Zintellect`

The most accessible federal research appointment on this board, and the one most
students overlook. NETL's work is carbon capture and storage, hydrogen,
gasification, materials for extreme environments and process systems — close to
straight chemical engineering. Two things make it unusual: there is **no blanket

### Sandia National Laboratories — Student Internships and Co-ops
`Albuquerque, NM · Livermore, CA · apply per-requisition`

Sandia runs three distinct student formats and they are on different clocks:
summer (10–12 weeks, May to the last Thursday in August), year-round (part-time
in term, full-time on breaks), and school-partnered co-ops of three to eight
months. For ChemE the work sits in energy storage, combustion, materials,
process safety and nuclear. The real gate is the clearance: most positions

requires US citizenship, and Sandia states that dual citizenship may affect your
ability to obtain one. There is no single application — you apply to each
requisition separately through the Student Job Family filter, and the postings
appear on their own schedules rather than in one fall wave.

### INROADS — Internship Program
`National · corporate partner placements · apply via inroads.org`

A talent-pipeline organisation rather than an employer: you apply to INROADS,
complete their interview-preparation and mock-interview requirements, and their
talent managers match you to a corporate partner's paid internship. For a
sophomore who is struggling to get traction with direct applications this is a
genuinely different channel, and the GPA floor of 2.8 is well below what the
refining and semiconductor requisitions ask for. Two constraints to plan around:
you must have at least one summer or 27 credit hours left before graduation, so
rising seniors are largely out, and INROADS does not cover relocation or
transportation — a placement in another state is on your budget. Fall priority
consideration closes 1 November 2026.

## Refining, midstream and energy

The only commercial sector with a meaningful number of 2027-labelled
requisitions actually open today. Phillips 66 has a full slate live, Marathon has
a Spring 2027 co-op posted across sixteen refineries, and Flint Hills has a
Summer intern req up. The universal disqualifier here is not GPA — it is work
authorization: every one of these requisitions excludes temporary visa holders
outright, several in language that also rules out future sponsorship. The second
disqualifier is physical: respirator fit means clean-shaven, and refinery sites
require a TWIC and a driver's licence. If you are eligible, this is where your
next hour goes, because these close when filled and nobody will tell you when
that happens.

## How to use this board

- **Do the two federal deadlines first, this week.** NASA OSTEM Spring 2027
  closes 14 September and DOE SULI Spring 2027 closes 30 September. Both require
  documents you cannot produce overnight — an official transcript showing credit
  hours as of the deadline, and recommendation letters. If you have not asked a
  professor yet, ask today; SULI's own applicant office hours on 9, 16 and 23
  September exist precisely because people get this wrong.
- **Treat September and October as the refining and chemicals window, not
  December.** Phillips 66, Marathon and Flint Hills have 2027 requisitions open
  now with no deadlines, which means they close silently when filled. Valero
  states outright that some positions are listed for as few as three days. A
  weekly portal check plus the career fair beats a monthly application push.
- **Search semiconductors and pharma by the work, not by your degree.** "Chemical
  engineering" returns almost nothing at a fab or a biologics company. Search
  *process engineer, process integration, yield, thin films, etch, CMP, wet
  clean* for fabs, and *process development, drug substance, drug product,
  technical operations, MSAT* for pharma. TSMC's Summer 2027 requisition names
  chemical engineering directly and covers twelve role families in one
  application.
- **Check the year in the title before you check anything else.** This board
  found a Phillips 66 req restricted to Chemistry rather than ChemE, an
  ExxonMobil req that still renders but is closed, a Koch req titled Summer 2027
  whose eligibility text points at Summer 2028, an Air Products requisition
  labelled FY 2026 sitting at the top of search results, and a Huntsman summer
  posting with no year at all. Fall is when stale postings look most current.
- **If you need a spring or fall term rather than a summer, look now — the
  spring-term postings are the ones closing first.** Marathon has a Spring 2027
  co-op across sixteen refineries, GE Vernova has a Spring 2027 nuclear co-op,
  ORNL's spring appointments close 28 October, and Hershey's two-term co-op
  starts this fall. Summer-only searching will miss all of them.
- **If you are not a US citizen or permanent resident, build your list from the
  short set that will actually take you.** Every federal programme here except
  NETL requires citizenship or permanent residency, and every refining
  requisition excludes temporary visa holders. NETL's PIP has no blanket
  citizenship bar and a 2.5 GPA floor; ExxonMobil's intake requisitions state no
  sponsorship policy either way; Kairos, GE Vernova and SpaceX are all
  export-controlled and effectively closed. Ask employers directly rather than
  inferring from silence.

## Caveats

**Pages that blocked or failed retrieval.** Nine URLs returned an error or an
empty body this session and their cards are badged accordingly: Micron's Workday
requisition JR107323 (empty body) and careers.micron.com job pages (platform
configuration only); Air Products' Workday requisition JR-2025-17688 (empty
body); Texas Instruments' Oracle requisition 25016565 (empty body); Jacobs'
Avature pages for reqs 43347 and 38208 and its intern search page (empty body,
three attempts); Marathon's own Workday page for req 00020447 (empty body —
facts read from a mirror instead); the University of Michigan Chemistry REU 2027
page, careers.tsmc.com's Avature listing, westlake.com's internship page, and
AIChE's WISE and RAPID pages (all HTTP 403); lanl.gov's undergraduate student
programme pages (HTTP 404); and Qnity Electronics' 2026-term co-op pages (HTTP
410 Gone). Modern Workday, Eightfold and Oracle career portals render entirely
in the browser, so a large fraction of real requisitions are simply unreadable to
automated retrieval — their absence from this board is a retrieval limitation,
not evidence that the job does not exist.

**Sectors that came up thin.** Environment, water and consulting is the weakest
section here: Jacobs was the only consultancy with 2027-dated postings found, and
none of its pages were readable. AECOM, Brown and Caldwell, Stantec, Black &
Veatch, CDM Smith and Veolia were not covered. Undergraduate research is thin for
a real reason — no ChemE REU site checked had opened its 2027 cycle, and at least
one flags that 2027 funding status is pending. Graduate and PhD routes are thin
because industrial PhD internships are largely arranged through advisor and
conference contacts rather than posted requisitions.

**What was not covered.** International programmes were not pursued beyond
noting that ExxonMobil has Singapore (1H 2027) and Kuala Lumpur chemical
engineering internship postings and that AstraZeneca runs UK-based chemical
engineering placements at Macclesfield — none were opened or characterised, and
none appear as cards. Canadian internships, Gulf-region programmes, and the AIChE
RAPID and WISE society-specific application routes were also left unverified
(both AIChE pages returned 403). Semiconductor equipment makers (Applied
Materials, Lam Research) surfaced only 2025- and 2026-dated postings and were
dropped rather than presented as current. Pulp and paper, cement, mining and
metals, and defence primes other than SpaceX were not searched.

**Two specific things to distrust.** The Koch/Flint Hills requisition 189408 is
titled Summer 2027 but its eligibility text and at least one index rendering
point at Summer 2028; and both Amgen Summer 2027 requisitions display a start
date of 30 August 2026, which is almost certainly a posting date but is not
disambiguated on the page. In both cases, ask the recruiter rather than assuming.

**Deadlines change, and the linked page is the authority.** Every date, pay
figure, GPA threshold and eligibility rule above was read from a page on the date
in the masthead and may have changed since. Requisitions with no stated deadline
close when filled and give no notice. Before you rely on anything here — before
you plan a semester around a spring co-op or skip a career fair because a
posting looked stale — open the link and read the requisition yourself.
