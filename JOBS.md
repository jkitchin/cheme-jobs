# Chemical Engineering Job Board

Faculty-search season is at its peak. Twelve verified US chemical engineering faculty searches have review or full-consideration dates between October 21 and December 1, and most say "open until filled," so applying early still matters. For BS graduates, the hiring is in energy and refining new-grad programs (Chevron, Phillips 66), specialty chemicals (Eastman), and plant-based programs at Cargill and Dennis Group. Most of these exclude anyone who will need visa sponsorship. Federal BS openings are scarce and close fast. The one live federal chemical engineering posting found is the USPTO examiner announcement, which closes Sunday.

## How to use this board

- **Faculty season peaks in the next four weeks.** Most review dates fall between Nov 2 and Dec 1. "Review begins" means your file is read in the first pass if it is complete, and letters count toward complete. Ask your letter writers this week. MIT and Stanford both treat missing letters as missing applications.
- **USAJOBS 0893 announcements live for days, not weeks.** Letterkenny's chemical engineer job was posted, canceled, reposted, and closed within 26 days, with a 50-applicant cap. Save a search on series 0893, 0819 and 1224 (patent examiner) with daily email alerts, and keep a federal-format resume ready so you can apply the same day. DOE's Recent Graduate General Engineer posting ran Aug 12–27 with a 150-applicant cap, which is a sign of how fast Pathways slots fill.
- **The big industry new-grad requisitions are open now, and the spring backfill comes later.** Chevron, Phillips 66, Eastman and Cargill fill most 2027 slots from fall campus cycles and their own intern pools. From February to April, look for refreshed "evergreen" requisitions like Micron's, plus EPC firms (Dennis Group), to fill remaining seats.
- **National-lab postdocs roll year-round, but the fellowship deadlines don't.** PNNL's Linus Pauling Fellowship (closed Oct 15, 2026) and LLNL's Lawrence Fellowship (closed Oct 1) are done for this cycle. NIST-NRC's next deadline is **Feb 1, 2027**, and you need a NIST adviser on board before then. The UC President's Postdoctoral Fellowship closes **Nov 1, 2026**.
- **Postdoc start dates are negotiable, and asking costs nothing.** Argonne and PNNL accept PhDs "soon to be completed" (PNNL: within 8 months). If you defend in spring 2027, apply now and negotiate a summer start.
- **If you need sponsorship, sort by the Citizen row first.** On this board the realistic paths are DOE-lab postdocs (except Argonne radiochemistry), faculty searches, Envision Energy, and the PhD programs that are silent on citizenship (Air Products, ExxonMobil). Confirm with the recruiter before investing in a long application.
